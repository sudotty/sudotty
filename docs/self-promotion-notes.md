# Self-Promotion Notes

This page keeps the GitHub profile honest, practical, and easy to read for overseas hiring teams.

## Core positioning

I am not positioning myself as a model researcher. I am positioning myself as a production-minded full-stack and backend engineer who can turn AI capabilities into working business systems.

## One-line story

I build reliability-first AI agent systems for knowledge work, tool use, business workflows, developer workflows, and evaluation.

## Why my background matters

Since 2012, I have worked across financial systems, tax systems, backend services, frontend applications, mobile apps, and internal tools. That background is useful for AI agent work because agents need more than prompts. They need data models, workflows, permissions, logs, evaluations, and clear user boundaries.

## What the portfolio should prove

- I can build practical AI systems, not only demos.
- I understand real business workflow constraints.
- I can write clear English technical documentation.
- I can design systems that are reviewable and measurable.
- I can connect AI to documents, tools, code, and operations.

## Best interview framing

Use this framing in interviews:

I am a full-stack and backend engineer focused on production-grade AI agent systems. My strength is connecting backend engineering, workflow design, and AI system reliability. I care about systems that can be inspected, reviewed, measured, and improved.

## What to avoid

- Do not overclaim production usage before the projects are complete.
- Do not describe every repo as a platform.
- Do not use vague words like revolutionary or next-generation.
- Do not hide that these are portfolio projects. Make them credible by being specific.

## What to emphasize

- Practical business cases.
- Clear MVP scope.
- Evidence, traces, review, and evaluation.
- Small working demos.
- English README quality.
- Consistent issue roadmaps.
